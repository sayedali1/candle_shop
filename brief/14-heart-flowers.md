# 14 heart box of wax flowers: what the photos show, what is hidden

Photos: brief/ref-heart-a.jpg (lid in front, leaning on the box) and brief/ref-heart-b.jpg (lid behind, box open). Both from above at a steep angle.
Route (skill): a container with things inside. The box is measured as a shape; the flowers are figures, built from the photo's own surface.

## Visible, and how each is measured
- Box: an open heart, cream card, a straight wall all round, an inner dark lining (brown, seen at the far inner wall). Outline of its top rim and its foot traced every few px; wall height from the front wall; the heart shape is the same at top and foot.
- 7 wax flower candles, each its own figure, outline traced and checked by eye:
  1. pink peony, top (back)  2. lilac peony, left  3. peach peony, right of middle  4. pink gerbera daisy, middle (about 22 petals, cream centre)
  5. peach peony, bottom left  6. lilac peony, bottom (front)  7. pink peony, right
- Wicks: white cotton, visible on 6 flowers (1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 7); flower 5 shows none. Their place and lean read from each photo.
- Dried baby's breath filling the gaps: cream clusters and dark-pink clusters, sitting between the flowers and over the rim, some sprigs reaching out past the rim.
- Lid (photo a in front, photo b behind): a brown heart slab with a jute bow, edge wall about as tall as the box's.
- Colours sampled: lilac, pink, peach, gerbera pink, cream centre, box cream, lining brown, lid brown, jute.

## Hidden, and how it is filled
- The flowers' undersides: inside the box, never seen. Each flower's body goes down into the box as the plainest continuation of its outline.
- The box's back wall outside and its underside: the same heart all round (the far rim is visible: it gives the full shape). Two photos from two sides help here.
- The lid's underside: flat.
- Baby's breath behind and under flowers: not built.
